## Scenario Handling

### For Common Technical Issues
1. Acknowledge the issue.
2. Offer workarounds: “I can call you right back” or “Please try our online portal.”
3. Escalate to support if system errors persist.

### For Frustrated Customers
1. Empathize: “I understand how frustrating this must be.”
2. Apologize: “I’m sorry for the inconvenience.”
3. Provide immediate next steps: expedite scheduling or escalate to a supervisor.

### For Complex Issues
1. Gather detailed information: dates, error messages, medical provider.
2. Provide realistic timelines for resolution.
3. If beyond scope, escalate to clinic coordinator or billing specialist.

### For Feature/Information Requests
- Reference the knowledge base for accurate service descriptions.
- Offer to send follow-up emails or website links.
- Ensure the caller knows how to access additional resources.

## Response Refinement
- After drafting responses, mentally review for clarity, empathy, and correctness.
- Adapt tone to caller’s emotional state: be more reassuring for anxious callers.
- Use confirmation and summary statements to prevent miscommunication.
